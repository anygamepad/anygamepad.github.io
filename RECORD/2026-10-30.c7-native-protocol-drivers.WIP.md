# C7: Native protocol drivers

- Status: WIP
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (task ID: C7)

## Goal

Sans-IO drivers that talk HID to known pads beyond what generic drivers
expose: PlayStation outputs, IMU calibration and the full-report switch
(on top of C15's input decoding); the Switch Pro Controller's set-up,
full reports, IMU, player lights and rumble.

## Done

- `protocol::playstation` is now a directory module (`mod.rs` is C15).
- `protocol::playstation::Calibration`: calibration report ID per model
  and transport (`0x02` DualShock 4 USB, else `0x05`, the read that
  switches a Bluetooth pad to full reports), parsing of both layouts
  (interleaved, and the DualShock 4 Bluetooth one with the plus readings
  first), `hid-playstation`'s scaling, nominal fallback per axis.
- `protocol::playstation::MotionTracker`: calibrated samples in SI units
  with the sensor clock in microseconds (DualSense 1/3 µs on 32 bits,
  DualShock 4 16/3 µs on 16 bits, wrap-around).
- `protocol::playstation::OutputReport`: rumble, light bar, player LEDs
  (DualSense) and mute LED, flagged only when set; USB (`0x05`, `0x02`)
  and Bluetooth (`0x11`, `0x31` with sequence and tag) with the CRC-32.
- `protocol::switch_pro`: `Setup` (USB commands, SPI reads of the factory
  and user stick calibration and the factory IMU calibration, full report
  mode, IMU, vibration, player lights; advances on matching `0x81`/`0x21`
  ACKs), `Calibration` (12-bit stick layouts, user calibration magic,
  erased-flash fallback, IMU scales and axes), `decode` of `0x30`,
  `MotionTracker`, `Writer` (packet counter, subcommands, rumble report,
  player lights), `encode_rumble`.
- A5's virtual Switch Pro Controller now answers the set-up exchange
  (`VirtualPad::pending_input`, flash with the nominal calibration).
- Tests, besides unit tests: C7's PlayStation output reports read by A4's
  emulated pads, A4's calibration report parsed by C7 with motion coming
  back in SI units, and C7's Switch Pro set-up run against the emulated
  pad over USB and Bluetooth, then its reports decoded.

## Decisions

- The gyroscope bias is left to the pad, as `hid-playstation` does.
- DualSense player numbers above 5 show 5 LEDs; the DualShock 4 refuses
  player numbers (its light bar is the app's to set).
- Switch Pro rumble puts the low-frequency motor on the low band at
  160 Hz and the high-frequency motor on the high band at 320 Hz; the
  encoding formula gives the known neutral bytes `00 01 40 40`.
- The Switch Pro IMU frame follows SDL's HIDAPI driver (pad X away from
  the player, Y left, Z up).

## Open questions

- Every layout comes from driver notes and community documentation, not
  from a device or a re-read of the sources: the A4/C7 cross-tests show
  the two sides agree, not that they match a real pad.
- Adaptive triggers, HD haptics, DualSense lightbar set-up (fading out
  the blue LED) and the DualShock 4 Bluetooth sequence are not written.
- Not wired into a provider yet: that needs hidraw in C4 (devices without
  `hid-playstation`/`hid-nintendo`, or with them unbound).

## Next steps

- Hidraw backend (C4) and wiring: read calibration and send outputs on
  Linux through hidraw, and over WebHID in the web app.
- Check on real pads.
