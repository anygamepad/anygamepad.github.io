# Tester: theme switch and darker DualSense sticks

- Status: completed
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (follow-ups to W8; no task of their own)

## Goal

Two owner requests on the web tester: sticks that stand out in the
DualSense drawing, and a theme switch (auto, light, dark).

## Done

- `art/dualsense.svg`: darker stick wells and caps (grey caps on a darker
  well instead of near-white on near-white).
- Theme switch in the header (Auto, Light, Dark; `aria-pressed`), wrapping
  under the tagline on phones. `theme.ts` sets `data-theme` on the root
  element and keeps the choice in `localStorage` (`anygamepad.theme`);
  `style.css` uses the dark tokens for `data-theme="dark"`, or for the
  system's dark scheme unless `data-theme="light"`, and sets
  `color-scheme` to match.
- The stored theme is applied before the WebAssembly loads, so the page
  does not first show the other theme.
- Smoke test: the default follows the system, a pick changes the colours,
  survives a reload, and Auto goes back to the system's scheme.

## Decisions

- The dark tokens are written twice (media query and attribute) rather than
  with `light-dark()`. Reason: `light-dark()` needs Safari 17.5; older
  browsers would lose every colour.
- Storage failures (private mode, blocked storage) are ignored: the choice
  then lasts until the page closes. Auto is not stored.
