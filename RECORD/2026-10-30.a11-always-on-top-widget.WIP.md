# A11: Always-on-top input widget

- Status: WIP
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (task ID: A11)

## Goal

A small window above other windows with one controller's live state:
borderless, optionally transparent and click-through, resizable, with a
selectable device and skins; platform limits handled and documented.

## Done

- `apps/desktop/src/widget.rs`: a second egui viewport (undecorated,
  transparent, always on top, out of the taskbar, resizable,
  click-through on request), dragged by its body while it takes clicks;
  two skins: a controller (triggers, shoulders, sticks, d-pad, face
  buttons, menus, home) and compact (sticks, triggers, names of the
  buttons pressed); name and battery on top.
- Settings: show, controller (the first one by default), skin, background
  opacity, click-through. The app's clear colour is transparent so the
  widget can be.
- `apps/desktop/README.md`: Wayland, X11, transparency and full-screen
  limits.
- Checked under Xvfb with the demo pad: the widget follows the pad live.
  Xvfb's OpenGL has no alpha configuration (the widget falls back to
  opaque, logged) and no window manager (always on top not applied), so
  both are untested.

## Decisions

- A viewport of the same app rather than a second process. Reason: it
  shares the device worker and repaints on the same changes.
- No layer-shell yet on Wayland. Reason: winit does not offer it; the
  documented fallbacks (window menu, XWayland) cover the use until a
  layer-shell backend is worth its weight.
- The widget's position and size are not saved yet.

## Open questions

- Behaviour on real desktops: KDE and GNOME on Wayland and X11, Windows,
  macOS (floating level over full-screen Spaces is not set yet).
