## Decisions

- F4 keeps blocking H6. Reason: the reports of the first campaign must
  carry unit codes for the inventory to be built from them; naming units
  is small and comes first. Reports made before (without a unit) still
  count for everything else.
- Machines are not built from reports. Reason: a report holds the OS,
  its version cut to numbers and the browser, never which computer; the
  inventory's machines stay hand-written, if wanted at all.
- W9 gets its own ID (W8 is the tester layout). Reason: task IDs are
  stable within a version and across RECORDs.
- A new version rather than an in-place edit. Reason: AGENTS.md allows
  in-place re-plans only on the version's own day (2026-10-31).
