## Decisions

- Proposals for F3 (not ADRs yet), each with its reason in the notes:
  - Arbitration follows SDL's model: ordered providers that claim devices by
    identity; default order native protocol driver > SDL3 HIDAPI > native
    generic > SDL3 generic > gilrs.
  - The SDL3 provider defaults `SDL_JOYSTICK_ENHANCED_REPORTS=auto` and feeds
    `SDL_HIDAPI_IGNORE_DEVICES` / `SDL_GAMECONTROLLER_IGNORE_DEVICES` with the
    devices the native provider owns. Reason: SDL's default changes
    PlayStation report mode until power-cycle and breaks DirectInput for
    other apps.
  - VID/PID are optional identity fields (XInput, Safari GameController
    devices hide them); the identity must be able to produce an SDL GUID.
  - The GameControllerDB importer translates SDL backend element indices to
    order-independent sources (evdev codes, HID usages).
- Upstream contributions to SDL and GameControllerDB must be human-authored.
  Reason: both projects forbid AI-generated contributions (SDL `AGENTS.md`,
  GameControllerDB README).
