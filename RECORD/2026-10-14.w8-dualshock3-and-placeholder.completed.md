# W8: DualShock 3 illustration, one stick colour, placeholder

- Status: completed
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (task ID: W8, follow-up)

## Goal

The owner's requests of 2026-10-14: use their DualShock 3 illustration
without its "DS3" text, give every drawing the DualSense's stick colour
(sticks of all of them; buttons and touchpad of the DualShock 4 as well),
and fill the empty detail column with a placeholder while no pad is
selected.

## Done

- `apps/web/src/art/dualshock3.svg`, built from the owner's file with its
  paths unchanged (a script read them out): L1/R1 are the shapes behind the
  body (with their L and R marks), the L2/R2 travel bars go above them
  (viewBox grown upwards), Select, Start, PS, the four D-pad arms and the
  face buttons are `data-button` groups, and the sticks keep their collar
  while the cap moves (travel 12, the design's small cap offset kept in an
  inner group). The "DS3" text, the C2PA metadata and the Spanish title and
  description are gone; comments are in English. The DualShock 3 shape
  layout is gone.
- Stick caps everywhere take the DualSense's colours (radial `#9ea3b5` to
  `#6a7083`, edge `#5a5f71`, inner ring `#8a90a3`): the DualShock 3 and
  Xbox sticks, and on the DualShock 4 the sticks, face buttons, D-pad,
  L1/R1, Share/Options, PS button and touchpad.
- Placeholder: while no pad is selected, the detail column shows the
  generic drawing at rest, grey and faded, in a dashed frame, with "The
  selected gamepad shows here, drawn live as you use it." It is decorative
  (`aria-hidden`) and uses its own class, so `.detail` still means a pad is
  shown.
- The build minifies the illustrations (`anygamepad:minified-art` in
  `vite.config.ts`: comments and whitespace out, `?raw` SVG imports only).
- Smoke test: the placeholder before any pad and after the last one leaves,
  none while a pad is shown; a DualShock 3 fixture (`054c:0268`) checks its
  drawing's controls and a lit Cross; the activity log check includes it.
  Passes in Chromium.
- Screenshots checked: the four illustrations with presses, and the
  placeholder at 1200 px (light) and 390 px (dark).
- Size (gzip): JS 57.0 KiB of 64 (58.5 before the minifier, 53.4 before the
  DualShock 3).

## Decisions

- "DS3" text removed rather than redrawn. Reason: the owner allowed either,
  and no logos or brand marks is the drawings' rule (W8).
- "The DualSense's stick colour" covers every dark part of the DualShock 4
  the owner listed (buttons includes the D-pad, bumpers, Share/Options and
  PS). Its touchpad keeps its dot pattern; the face symbols keep their
  colours.
- The placeholder is the generic drawing, not a new image. Reason: no extra
  bytes, and it shows what the page will do.
- Minifying the illustrations at build time rather than in the files.
  Reason: the sources keep the markup documentation; 1.5 KiB less.

## Open questions

- The DualShock 4's blue cross on the new grey has less contrast than
  before; the owner may prefer lighter symbols.
