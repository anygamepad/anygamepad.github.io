# Desktop app in the web app's style

- Status: completed
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (follow-up to A1, A2, A3, A11)

## Goal

The owner asked (2026-10-30) for the desktop app to look like the web
app.

## Done

- `apps/desktop/src/style.rs`: the colour tokens of `apps/web/src/style.css`
  for light and dark (`--bg`, `--fg`, `--muted`, `--card`, `--border`,
  `--accent`, `--error`), radii (6 px controls, 8 px cards), spacing and
  text sizes; outlined accent buttons, accent selection; `card()` (border,
  accent border when selected) and `meter()` (the web's thin track); the
  system UI font loaded from the usual paths per OS (Segoe UI, San
  Francisco, Cantarell, Noto Sans, DejaVu Sans), as the web's `system-ui`.
- Header: logo and two-tone wordmark (`--brand-muted`), tabs, and the
  Auto/Light/Dark switch at the right as on the web.
- Devices list as cards with the selected one in accent; trigger and raw
  axis bars as meters; lit controls in the accent colour; settings and
  collection sections in cards; the widget's lit colour is the dark
  accent.
- Checked with screenshots in both themes under Xvfb.

## Decisions

- The system font is read from disk, not embedded. Reason: it is what
  `system-ui` gives on the web, and embedding fonts would grow the binary;
  egui's own font stays the fallback.
