# Drop the back buttons from the controller drawings

- Status: completed
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (follow-up to W8)

## Goal

The generic drawing showed four back buttons below the grips at full
strength on every unrecognized pad, as if the pad had them.

## Done

- `apps/web/src/art/xbox.svg`: the four back buttons are removed and the
  viewBox ends below the grips.
- `apps/web/src/layouts.ts`: the Xbox layout no longer hides them (nothing
  to hide); comments updated. `ArtLayout.hide` stays for later drawings.
- Smoke test updated; passes in Chromium.

## Decisions

- No back buttons in any drawing. Reason: pads map them to front buttons
  (owner, 2026-10-30). The canonical model keeps `left_paddle_*` and
  `right_paddle_*` for modes that report them on their own.
