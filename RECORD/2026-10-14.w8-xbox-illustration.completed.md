# W8: Xbox illustration

- Status: completed
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (task ID: W8, follow-up)

## Goal

Use the owner's Xbox illustration (Series X|S, with C and Z buttons added
for pads such as the Flydigi Vader 5 Pro) for every pad with that layout:
the Xbox family and the generic drawing.

## Done

- `apps/web/src/art/xbox.svg`, from the owner's file, marked up like the
  DualSense: one `data-button` group per control with `.shape` and `.mark`
  parts (Xbox, View, Menu, Share, face buttons, C as `extra_0`, Z as
  `extra_1`), sticks whose cap moves (`data-stick`, `data-cap`, travel 10)
  and lights whole on L3/R3, and the D-pad's middle facets as its four arms.
  Comments in English; the C2PA metadata block, the Spanish title and
  description were dropped (the edits void the signature, and a root
  `<title>` would become the drawing's tooltip).
- Added to the design: LB/RB as the top edge above the existing seams (RB
  mirrors LB), LT/RT travel bars above them, and the four back buttons as
  pills below the grips, as the generic drawing had them. The viewBox grows
  to fit them (`8 80 698 538`).
- `layouts.ts`: the Xbox family and the generic layout use the
  illustration; the shape layouts of both and `OFFSET_BODY` are gone. An
  `ArtLayout` can `hide` controls (the Xbox family hides the back buttons)
  and `dim` optional ones.
- C and Z are optional controls (`data-optional`): dimmed (35% opacity,
  titled "not on this pad", never lit) on pads that do not report
  `extra_0`/`extra_1` (from the Gamepad API capabilities), and always on
  the Xbox family, at the owner's request (first version hid them).
- `drawing.ts`: a mark with `data-label` shows only when the pad's
  `FaceLayout` gives its button that label. Xbox pads show A/B/X/Y; generic
  pads, whose labels are unknown, show blank face buttons, as the shape
  drawing did.
- Smoke test: the Xbox pad's labels and controls (no back buttons, C and Z
  dimmed), the generic pad's controls with C and Z lit by `extra_0`, a
  17-button generic pad with C and Z dimmed; the DualShock 4 check lists
  its controls too. Passes in Chromium; Firefox and WebKit run in CI.
- Screenshots checked at 1200 px (light) and 390 px (dark): at rest,
  pressed (LB, RT, A, D-pad, View, Xbox, L3, sticks tilted, values shown)
  and the generic pad with C, Y, RB and LT.
- Sizes (gzip): JS 51.7 KiB of 64 (48.1 before), wasm and CSS unchanged.

## Decisions

- One illustration for both the Xbox family and generic pads. Reason: the
  owner asked for it for every pad with that layout; the generic drawing
  already followed the Vader 5 Pro's Xbox-style layout.
- Controls a family lacks are removed or dimmed per layout, not drawn in a
  second file. Reason: one file to maintain. Xbox pads have no back buttons
  (the Elite's paddles are not told apart yet).
- C and Z are dimmed, not hidden, where a pad lacks them (owner,
  2026-10-14): the drawing shows the layout and what this pad does not
  have. The Xbox family dims them whatever the pad reports, because
  Chromium reports an Xbox Series pad's share button as `extra_0`.
- Only C and Z are optional for now. Reason: other extra buttons are still
  guesses (Chromium appends the touchpad click of PlayStation pads as
  `extra_0`), so dimming every unreported control would dim controls pads
  do have; device profiles (D4) will make this exact.
- Face letters follow the library's `FaceLayout`, not the artwork. Reason:
  unknown pads can be labelled differently (Nintendo-style, numbers); the
  shape drawings already worked this way.
- The Switch Pro Controller keeps its shape drawing. Reason: same positions
  but other labels (Nintendo) and buttons (capture, no share); it can use
  the illustration later with Nintendo marks if the owner wants.
- Illustrations are still bundled in the main chunk. Reason: the budget
  sums every JS file in `dist/`, so loading on demand would lighten the
  first load but not the budget; 12.5 KiB remain.
