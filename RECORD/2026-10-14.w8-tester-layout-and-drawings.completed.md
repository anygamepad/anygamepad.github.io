# W8: Tester layout and device drawings

- Status: completed
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (task ID: W8)

## Goal

A tester page in the spirit of `ds.daidr.me`: pads on the left, the
selected one drawn large on the right, with a drawing per controller
family, as the owner asked.

## Done

- Layout: two columns from 56 rem (pads and the WebHID box on the left, the
  selected pad's detail on the right), one column on phones. Pads are
  buttons; the first one is selected, and a removed selection falls back to
  the next pad.
- Drawings (`layouts.ts` as data, `drawing.ts` renders): DualShock 3,
  DualShock 4 (light bar, touchpad), DualSense and Edge (touchpad, mute),
  Switch Pro (capture), Xbox (share), and a generic drawing after the
  Flydigi Vader 5 Pro (C and Z as `extra_0` and `extra_1`, four back
  buttons as the canonical paddles). Face buttons show their labels from the
  library's `FaceLayout`: letters, or PlayStation symbols drawn as shapes.
  Every control is titled with its canonical name.
- "Show values" puts trigger and stick values on the drawing.
- Raw buttons and axes are folded under the drawing, open when there is no
  drawing (no standard mapping).
- The live view marks which pad it shows (`data-view`), so tests wait for
  the rebuild after a selection change.
- Smoke test rewritten for the list, selection, family drawings and labels,
  values, vibration and hotplug. Screenshots checked for every family at
  1200 px (light) and 390 px (dark).

## Decisions

- One renderer and layouts as data. Reason: six drawings that differ in
  outline and positions only; a new family is a new object.
- The family comes from the library (`Family`), the drawing from the page.
  Reason: identification is library logic and will move to profiles (D4);
  shapes are presentation.
- C and Z are drawn on `extra_0` and `extra_1`. Reason: a guess that holds
  for the standard mapping's order; profiles will say which extra is which.
- Own outlines, no logos, PlayStation symbols as plain shapes. Reason:
  daidr's artwork is MIT but brand-like; simple outlines are enough to
  recognize the family.
- The DualShock 3 family came from the owner's `jgermade/sixaxis-pairing`
  (`054c:0268`).
