# Logo

- Status: completed
- Roadmap: ROADMAP/2026-10-14/ (no task; web app branding)

## Goal

Use the logo the owner chose (2026-10-30): a ring in two gradient arcs
around a white controller, and the word "AnyGamepad" in two tones.

## Done

- `docs/brand/logo.png`: the owner's image, as given (README header).
- `apps/web/public/favicon.svg`: the mark redrawn as SVG (favicon, and the
  mark in the tester's and privacy page's headers), colours sampled from
  the image.
- Headers: mark plus "Any" (text colour) and "Gamepad" (`--brand-muted`,
  slate in each theme) as HTML text, bold system font.
- Checked in Chromium screenshots, light, dark and phone width; smoke test
  passes.

## Decisions

- The mark is a vector redraw, not the PNG. Reason: crisp at favicon size
  and on any screen density, and small; the PNG has a dark background.
- A grey outline around the controller body, which the image does not have.
  Reason: the white body disappears on the light theme.
- The wordmark is text in the system font, not the image's lettering.
  Reason: the CSP allows no external fonts and the image's font is not
  known; text also follows the theme.
- Elsewhere the project name stays "anygamepad" (lowercase, as in AGENTS.md
  and the crate names); "AnyGamepad" only in the logo.

## Open questions

- A vector source of the logo from the owner would replace the redraw, and
  the font name would let the wordmark match.
